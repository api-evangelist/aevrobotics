# Applied EV (formerly AEV Robotics)

> Applied EV — legal entity Applied Electric Vehicles Ltd — is an Australian software-defined
> vehicle company founded in Melbourne in 2015. It builds the Blanc Robot cabinless autonomous
> electric vehicle platform, the Digital Backbone ASIL-D programmable vehicle control system, and
> an Internet-of-Vehicles (IoV) Cloud Platform that the company markets as API-first. The company
> was previously known as AEV Robotics; appliedev.com is the current and only live domain
> (aevrobotics.com no longer resolves).

## Important for agents

- **There is no public API.** Applied EV markets vehicle APIs but publishes no developer portal,
  no API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no SDK and no Postman collection that a
  member of the public can reach. Do not attempt to construct requests against these APIs from the
  marketing descriptions below — no base URL, authentication scheme, schema or error contract has
  been published.
- The developer host `developer.appliedev.com` and the identity host `sso.appliedev.com` exist in
  DNS and in Certificate Transparency but do not accept public connections. Access is presumed to
  require a customer tenant or a network allowlist.
- Access begins with a sales conversation at https://www.appliedev.com/?contact=true

## Products

- [Blanc Robot](https://www.appliedev.com/vehicles): Cabinless, adaptable autonomous electric
  vehicle engineered for logistics and industrial transport. 6th generation launched December 2024
  with Suzuki as key OEM partner; deployed in Japan from September 2025.
- [Digital Backbone](https://www.appliedev.com/sdv): Proprietary safety-rated, fully programmable
  vehicle control system delivering scalable autonomy for a vehicle or task. Gen 6 launched at
  CES 2023 with multiple layers of redundancy targeting SAE Level 4 and Level 5 applications.
- [Vehicle Integration](https://www.appliedev.com/integration): Integration of the Digital Backbone
  and Blanc Robot platform into third-party vehicles and autonomous driving stacks.
- SDV components: Central Control Unit (CCU), DC-DC Converter, Low-Voltage Power Distribution Unit
  (LVPDU), Steering Motor Control Unit (SMCU) — https://www.appliedev.com/products/ccu

## IoV Cloud Platform (marketed API surface — not publicly documented)

Source: https://www.appliedev.com/cloud — names, one-line descriptions and grouping are quoted
from that page. No operations, paths, parameters, schemas, scopes or error codes are published.

- **Access Manager** — identity and access management for all vehicle operators.
- **Vehicle Task API** — delegating tasks to a vehicle.
- **Vehicle Mission API** — executing task sequences and logic.
- **Drive API** — autonomous driving and ASIL-D motion control.
- **Pod & Accessory API** — connect and safely control vehicle pods and accessories.
- **Virtual Vehicles** — simulated vehicle technologies for development and testing.
- **Vehicle Management System (VMS)** — built on the IoV Cloud Platform APIs; monitors vehicle
  status, location, telemetry and diagnostics, and manages vehicle software and over-the-air
  updates. Provides IAM, mapping, mission creation and vehicle command, designed for extensibility
  and third-party system integration.
